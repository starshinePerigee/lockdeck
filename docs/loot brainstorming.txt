Loot is the following options:

- coins: used for repairs and buying at the shop
- ingots: give you new picks
- potions: repair broken picks
- artifacts: single-use consumables
- gems: pick effect upgrades
- jewelry: pick special effects
- bones: win the game


Every loot claim window shows a picture of the loot with a label. This always takes 1 card worth of space.

- if you press the continue button twice, it loots all coins

How much loot is there? 

(note: all values have since been 5x'ed)

Let's start with some fixed values. 
- The minimum is one coin. 
- Repairing a pick is baselined at 2
- Breaking and repairing a pick twice is 5, thrice is 9

Expect one or two breaks per lock. At a three lock set:
- early game locks, 1 break / lock, means 6 coins repair fees
- late game locks, 2 break / lock at already broken - ~20 coins repair fees

So start at 10 coins, and end at 30, adding 5 each round. Artifacts, ingots, gems, and jewelry are all few coins (average 3)
- earlygame (10): 5 coins, 1 gem, 1 artifact
- midgame (20): 1 ingot (4), 2 gem (2ea), 1 jewelry (3), 2 artifacts (2ea), 11 coins
- endgame (30): 2 ingots (4ea), 2 gem (2ea), 1 jewelry (3), 3 artifacts (2ea), 2 potions (2ea), 9 coins

Start at 10 and add five each round up to 30, then add 2 per round after.
