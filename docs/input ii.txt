pins, cards, discard, end turn are "selectable objects".
- their draw state is exclusively managed by game_core
- inputs are managed and activated exclusively by game_core

droppables implement:
- get_drop_area
- core_highlight
- core_unhighlight

clickables implement:
- get_

all activatable objects have the following:
- contains_point()
- collides_with()
- core_select
- core_deselect (which functions as reset / drag halt)
- core_click
