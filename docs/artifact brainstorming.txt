candlestick: add two segments to the candle
chalice: draw five cards
dagger: advance and set first unset pin
spyglass: reveal next three depths for all pins
crown: gain a coin for every broken pick this round
hourglass: invincible for three turns
scepter: jam 2 all pins (if special jam is available: do not block test and reveal)
orb: skip this stage; does not work on boss locks
silverware: gives you five toothpicks
