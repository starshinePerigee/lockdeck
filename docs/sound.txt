Sound is composed of ambience, music, and effects

Ambience: three channels
- softly crackling torch
- bugs 1
- bugs 2

ambience is always running, with crackle on a loop.

shortly after the game starts, one of the bugs comes in - from then on, we have a random (5-15 second) check for both bugs channels:
- if no bugs are playing, pick a bugs at random and go
- if a bugs is already playing, 1/3 chance of picking a different bugs and going 

Music: one channel

Music matches the game state:
- menus: no music
- game: bass
- strats: guitar

like bugs, music has a random clock (after each pulse, look ahead 5-15 seconds). If the music doesn't match the current state, fade the current track and queue the correct one.

Music pulls randomly from the collection of "songs"

When you open the in-game menu, the music gets ducked.

Once you start playing with sounds, all channels get ducked all the way so samples can be played.
