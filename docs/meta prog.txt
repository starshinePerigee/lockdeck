THE BIG LEVEL REFACTOR
core idea: lots of different depths in a level is confusing and hard, and should be saved for the very sickos side.

However, big locks with lots of pins is interesting, even if they're mostly empty. Thus:
- more empty pins - add additional easy levels, easy 3-pins, etc.
- less depths all the time (but more copies is fine)
- a depth should be able to stand on its own (with break)
- difficulty works by moving from weaknesses > inconveniences > hazards
