All depth templates fall in one of three categories:
- Helpful
- Annoying
- Critical

The key break between annoying and critical is how much brainspace they take: a critical depth should be considered before any lock, but you can play annoying and helpful depths reactively. (A Jam is annoying and pushing into a Jam isn't too bad, and you'll only think about it when you pop a Caution, whereas a Labyrinth is something you have to think about every single pick with reveal)

The depths per five should be tweaked per template to keep them balanced within their bands. 


Level gen should start with a target number of templates, and then "bump" it a number of times to distribute difficulty. Bumps take the following:
-1 helpful
+1 annoy
-1 annoy +1 critical
nothing (lucky!)

if the req isn't available, that bump is skipped.
